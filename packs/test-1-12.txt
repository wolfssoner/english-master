#משפטים
Read the passage carefully before you answer the questions. | קרא את הקטע בקפידה לפני שאתה עונה על השאלות.
The second passage was longer than the first one. | הקטע השני היה ארוך יותר מהראשון.
The robot can perform a simple task many times. | הרובוט יכול לבצע משימה פשוטה פעמים רבות.
Each student received a different task. | כל תלמיד קיבל משימה אחרת.
The hospital uses robots to carry supplies. | בית החולים משתמש ברובוטים כדי לשאת אספקה.
My sister works in a big hospital. | אחותי עובדת בבית חולים גדול.
The nurse checked the patient every hour. | האחות בדקה את המטופל כל שעה.
A good nurse needs a lot of patience. | אח טוב צריך הרבה סבלנות.
The patient was ready to go home. | המטופל היה מוכן ללכת הביתה.
The nurses spend a lot of time with each patient. | האחיות מקדישות הרבה זמן לכל מטופל.
The robot moved slowly in the hall. | הרובוט נע לאט במסדרון.
The hall was crowded in the morning. | המסדרון היה צפוף בבוקר.
The machine can perform the same task without a mistake. | המכונה יכולה לבצע את אותה משימה בלי טעות.
She performed well on the second test. | היא ביצעה היטב את המבחן השני.
The robot saw its own reflection in the window. | הרובוט ראה את ההשתקפות שלו בחלון.
A reflection on the floor confused the sensor. | השתקפות על הרצפה בלבלה את החיישן.
The river near the city is very long. | הנהר ליד העיר ארוך מאוד.
After the rain, the water in the river went up. | אחרי הגשם, המים בנהר עלו.
The flood damaged many houses. | השיטפון פגע בבתים רבים.
Strong rain can flood the streets. | גשם כבד יכול להציף את הרחובות.
There was a clear improvement in her results. | היה שיפור ברור בתוצאות שלה.
Every small improvement is important. | כל שיפור קטן חשוב.
A good learner asks questions. | לומד טוב שואל שאלות.
The application helps each learner at his own speed. | האפליקציה עוזרת לכל לומד בקצב שלו.
Find a supporting detail in the second paragraph. | מצא פרט תומך בפסקה השנייה.
Each supporting detail explains the main idea. | כל פרט תומך מסביר את הרעיון המרכזי.
The robot followed a planned route. | הרובוט הלך לפי מסלול מתוכנן.
The bus did not follow the planned route. | האוטובוס לא נסע לפי המסלול המתוכנן.
Some decisions require human judgment. | חלק מההחלטות דורשות שיקול דעת אנושי.
A machine cannot replace human judgment. | מכונה לא יכולה להחליף שיקול דעת אנושי.
The water level of the river went up quickly. | מפלס המים של הנהר עלה מהר.
A sensor measures the water level every minute. | חיישן מודד את מפלס המים כל דקה.
She was disappointed with her first result. | היא הייתה מאוכזבת מהתוצאה הראשונה שלה.
Do not be disappointed after one mistake. | אל תתאכזב אחרי טעות אחת.
The change was gradual, not sudden. | השינוי היה הדרגתי, לא פתאומי.
We saw a gradual improvement in his English. | ראינו שיפור הדרגתי באנגלית שלו.
The actual result was different from the prediction. | התוצאה בפועל הייתה שונה מהתחזית.
The actual cause of the error was simple. | הסיבה הממשית לשגיאה הייתה פשוטה.
His result remained unchanged after a week. | התוצאה שלו נשארה ללא שינוי אחרי שבוע.
The price was unchanged. | המחיר נשאר ללא שינוי.
Guided practice helps new learners. | תרגול מונחה עוזר ללומדים חדשים.
The students did a guided exercise with the teacher. | התלמידים עשו תרגיל מונחה עם המורה.
This detail is irrelevant to the question. | הפרט הזה לא רלוונטי לשאלה.
Ignore irrelevant information. | התעלם ממידע לא רלוונטי.
Read the instructions carefully. | קרא את ההוראות בקפידה.
He checked the data carefully. | הוא בדק את הנתונים בזהירות.
The sensor measures the temperature accurately. | החיישן מודד את הטמפרטורה במדויק.
Answer the questions accurately. | ענה על השאלות במדויק.
Repeated practice improves memory. | תרגול חוזר משפר את הזיכרון.
After repeated attempts, he succeeded. | אחרי ניסיונות חוזרים, הוא הצליח.
The second explanation was clearer. | ההסבר השני היה ברור יותר.
The picture became clearer after the update. | התמונה נעשתה ברורה יותר אחרי העדכון.
She practices English regularly. | היא מתרגלת אנגלית באופן קבוע.
The system checks the water level regularly. | המערכת בודקת את מפלס המים באופן קבוע.
The hall was crowded with people. | המסדרון היה צפוף באנשים.

#סיפור Feedback That Helps and Feedback That Hurts | קשה
Almost every teacher and manager gives feedback, but not all feedback contributes to improvement. Researchers who examined thousands of cases found a surprising result: in about one third of them, feedback actually reduced performance. How can something that is designed to help make people worse?

The answer depends on what the feedback focuses on. Feedback that focuses on the person, such as "You are not good at this," tends to reduce motivation. The learner becomes disappointed and often stops making an effort. Feedback that focuses on the task, such as "In this paragraph, the evidence does not support your main idea," is much more effective. It gives the learner specific information that he can use in the next attempt.

Timing also matters. Feedback is most useful when it arrives soon after the activity, while the learner still remembers what he did. Feedback that arrives weeks later is often irrelevant, because the learner has already moved on to a different task. In addition, feedback should be clear. A comment such as "Improve your writing" does not explain what to change, whereas "Use shorter sentences in the first section" allows the learner to act immediately.

Finally, effective feedback compares the learner's performance with a goal, not with other people. When students are compared with the majority of the class, those with the lowest results often feel that progress is impossible. When they are compared with their own previous results, they can see gradual improvement, and this helps them maintain their persistence.

#סיפור Will Robots Replace Us? | קשה

#סיפור Can You Concentrate in a Crowded World? | קשה

No method is perfect. But in a world that is crowded with information, the ability to maintain attention may be one of the most valuable skills that a student can develop.

#סיפור Why Learning Feels Frustrating | בינוני
Have you ever studied a list of words, felt that you knew them all, and then forgot most of them the next morning? It is frustrating, but it is completely normal. In fact, it may be a sign that you are learning in a useful way.

Another helpful method is to spread your practice over time. Ten minutes a day for a week works better than one hour the night before a test. You may feel that your progress is slow, but slow progress is not necessarily bad progress.

So the next time you forget a word that you studied yesterday, do not give up. Try again tomorrow. Eventually, the word will stop being new, and it will become yours.

#סיפור Words That Grow | בינוני
Many English words are built from smaller parts. If you know the parts, you can often guess the meaning of a new word. The beginning un- usually means "not": unfamiliar means not familiar, and unpopular means not popular. In- and im- can also mean "not", as in incomplete, incorrect, inexpensive and impractical.

The beginning re- usually means "again": to rewrite is to write again. Dis- often means the opposite, as in dislike. Endings are useful too. The ending -ful means "full of", so helpful means full of help, while -less means "without", so careless means without care.

Finally, the ending -ly often turns a describing word into a way of doing something: quick becomes quickly, and confident becomes confidently.
