#סיפור The Computer That Became Slow | קשה
Dan works as a developer in a small team. One morning, his computer became very slow. When he clicked on a button, the screen did not change for almost a minute. Dan was nervous, because he had an important job to finish before noon.

First, Dan thought that the computer had malware. He ran a program that searches for malware, but it found nothing. Then he thought that the computer was simply too old. "I need a new computer," he told his team. However, a new computer is expensive, so the administrator, Tamar, decided to check the problem first.

Tamar opened a window that shows every program and how much memory it uses. She noticed something strange. There were more than 40 programs open, and Dan did not use most of them. One small program for pictures used a huge amount of memory because of an error in its code. Each hour it took more memory, and it never gave the memory back.

Tamar closed the program and asked Dan to turn the computer off and on again. After that, the computer was fast again. Tamar also deleted the program and told the developer of the program about the error.

Dan was relieved, but he was also a little sad that he had not checked the details first. "The computer is not old," Tamar explained. "The problem was one program with a memory error. Before you buy something new, try to find the real cause." Dan smiled. He understood that a quick decision is not always the correct decision.

#סיפור A Message That Looked Official | קשה
Noa is a student. On Thursday evening, she received a message on her phone. The message looked official. It had the name of her school and a picture of the school. It said: "Your information is incomplete. Click the button and enter your name and your PIN before Friday, or you will not have access to your homework."

Noa was anxious. Her homework was important, and Friday was very soon. She almost clicked the button. Then she noticed some strange details. First, the message did not come from the usual school phone. Second, the school never asks students for their PIN. Third, the message had some mistakes in its English.

Noa did not click. Instead, she called her teacher, Yossi. Yossi explained that the message was phishing. "Phishing is a trap," he said. "The message looks real, but its purpose is to steal your information. When someone has your PIN, he can log in as you."

The next morning, Yossi told the whole class about the message. He learned that three other students had received the same message, and one boy had clicked the button and entered his PIN. The administrator changed the boy's PIN quickly, so the problem did not become serious.

Yossi gave the class simple instructions. Do not click on a button in a suspicious message. Do not share your PIN. And when you are not sure, stop and ask a teacher. Noa felt happy that she had stopped and checked the details before she acted.

#סיפור The File That Nobody Could Open | קשה
Ron is a writer. For eight months, he worked on a long story on his computer. He kept the whole story in one single file. One evening, the power went off while the file was open. When the power came back, Ron tried to open the file, but the computer showed an error message: "The file is corrupted and cannot be opened."

Ron felt terrible. Eight months of work were inside that file. He tried to open it with three other programs, but every program showed the same error. He searched on websites for a solution and found many ideas, but most of them were not useful. Some websites offered a program that could "fix any file" for a high price. Ron almost paid, but he was not sure that these websites were reliable.

Finally, Ron called his sister, Maya, who works with data. Maya explained the cause. "When the power went off, the computer was still storing the file. Part of the file was stored, and part of it was not. As a result, the file became corrupted."

Maya could not fix the whole file, but she found an older file of the story in the storage of Ron's phone. Ron had sent the story to his phone two weeks before. So only two weeks of work were missing.

Now Ron keeps his story in two places: on his computer and on a second device. He also stores it in a new file every week. "I learned a hard lesson," he says. "One file in one place is a big risk."

#סיפור When the Firewall Said No | קשה
A small team of developers worked on a new website for a store. On Thursday morning, nobody in the team could open the website from their computers at work. Users at home could open it without problems, but the team could not.

The developers thought that the website was broken. They spent two hours checking their code, but they found no error. Then Omer, the new developer in the team, had an idea. "I think the problem is not in the website," he said. "I think the problem is in our system at work."

Omer was right. The day before, the administrator had changed the instructions of the firewall. A firewall is a program that decides which data can enter a system and which data cannot. The new instructions were too strong. As a result, the firewall stopped the data that came from the new website, because the website was new and unfamiliar.

The administrator was busy with another issue, so the team had to wait. Finally, in the afternoon, the administrator gave the website permission, and the developers could open it again.

At the end of the day, the team discussed the problem, and they made two decisions. First, the administrator will inform the team before he changes the firewall. Second, when there is a problem, the team will check the whole route of the data, and not only their own code. "We looked at our code for two hours, but the code was perfect," said Omer with a smile.

#סיפור Why You Should Update Your Software | קשה
Many people see an update notification on their phone or computer and click "Not now". They think that updates take too much time. They want to continue their work, and the update can wait. However, this decision can be a serious risk.

Software is complex, and every program has mistakes in its code. Some of these mistakes are small: a button in a strange place, or a word that is not correct. Other mistakes are more serious. They are vulnerabilities: weak parts in the software that someone with bad purposes can use to enter the system. When the developers of the software find a vulnerability, they fix it and send an update to all users.

Here is the problem. When developers send an update, people with bad purposes can also learn about the vulnerability. They know that many users will not update their software quickly. As a result, a user who waits a month can have a higher risk than before the update.

There are also other reasons to update. Updates can make programs faster, fix errors that cause programs to stop, and help software work with new devices.

Still, updates are not perfect. Once in a while, an update has its own errors. For this reason, important systems are usually updated in steps: first a few computers, and then all the others. For a normal user, however, the writer recommends a simple step: choose to update automatically, and do not wait too long.

#סיפור An Invalid Value in the Database | קשה
Yael is a developer at a store that has a website for books. One Friday, users began to send angry messages. They could not purchase books. Every time they clicked "Buy", they saw a message: "Error: invalid value."

Yael checked the code of the website, but the code had not changed for a week. Then she looked at the database, where the store keeps information about every book: its name, its price, and how many are in the store. She noticed something unusual in one row. The price of one book was the word "ten" instead of the value 10.

Yael found the cause quickly. A person in the store team had changed the price by hand the day before, and he entered the word instead of the value. The database stored the word without a warning. But the function that works with prices needs a value, not a word. When the function received the word, it could not continue, so it stopped and showed an error.

The strange part was that one bad row stopped the purchase of every book, not only that book. The reason was that the function read all the rows before each purchase. So one invalid value in one row could stop the whole store.

Yael fixed the row in a minute. However, she knew that the real problem was bigger: the database should not allow a word in the price. So she changed the database. Now the price must be a value, and if someone enters a word, the system shows a warning and does not store it. "A small mistake by one person should not stop the whole store," Yael told her team.

#סיפור Is the Information on a Website Correct? | קשה

Why is there so much inaccurate information? First, every person can create a website, and nobody checks the information before people read it. Second, some websites make money from clicks. For these websites, a surprising title is more important than a correct story. Third, some writers make mistakes, and other websites repeat these mistakes again and again.

A reliable article explains where its information comes from. Third, compare. If three reliable websites say the same thing, the information is probably correct. If only one website says it, be careful. Finally, notice your feelings. If a title makes you very angry or very excited, stop for a minute. Strong feelings can make people share a story before they check it.

These steps take a little time. But they can prevent you from believing, and sharing, information that is not correct.

Maya found two reasons. First, when she took one picture, she often took ten, and she kept all of them. Second, her message program stored every picture that her friends sent, and also pictures that she only looked at once.

Maya moved her old pictures to her computer and deleted the pictures that were almost the same. She also changed the instructions of the message program, so it does not store every picture automatically. Now her phone has a lot of free storage, and it is fast again.

#שאלה Which reason for the problem is NOT in the text? | Maya downloaded too many programs | Maya kept ten pictures of the same thing | The message program stored every picture from friends | Maya kept pictures that she looked at only once | תוכנות רבות מדי לא מוזכרות כסיבה.
#שאלה What did Maya do to solve the problem? | She moved old pictures, deleted pictures that were almost the same, and changed the message program | She bought a new phone | She deleted all her pictures | She stopped taking pictures | היא העבירה, מחקה ושינתה הגדרה. לא מחקה הכול ולא קנתה טלפון.

#סיפור The Variable Nobody Checked | בינוני
A team of developers created a program for a travel website. One of its functions sends a notification to each traveler one day before the trip. The function used a variable with the name "day", and for eight months it worked perfectly.

Then travelers began to send angry messages. They received the notification after their trip, not before it. The team searched for the cause for two days. Finally, they found it. A new developer had created another variable with the same name, "day", in another part of the code. His variable changed the value, and the function used an invalid value.

The team fixed the problem and made a new decision about the code: every variable must have a clear and special name, such as "trip_day". "A short name is quick to create," the oldest developer said, "but a clear name can prevent a long search."

#שאלה What was the cause of the problem? | A new developer created another variable with the same name | The function was deleted | Travelers changed the value | The website was slow | מפתח חדש יצר משתנה נוסף באותו שם, והוא שינה את הערך.
#שאלה Why did the team need two days to find the cause? | This information is not in the text | The code was corrupted | The new developer was on a trip | The travelers did not explain the problem | שאלה מטעה: הטקסט אומר שהחיפוש לקח יומיים, אבל לא מסביר למה. לכן התשובה היא שהמידע לא מופיע.
#שאלה What does the oldest developer want to say? | A clear name takes a little more time but can prevent a long search | Short names are always correct | Developers should not create variables | New developers make the most mistakes | שם ברור עולה מעט זמן עכשיו ויכול לחסוך חיפוש ארוך אחר כך.

#סיפור The Door With a Code | בינוני
A family bought a new door for their house. It opens with a code instead of a key. At first, everyone loved it. Nobody needed to carry a key, and nobody could forget it.

But one cold evening, the power in the whole street went off. The door needed power to open, and the family waited outside for an hour in the rain. Finally, the power came back, and the door opened.

After that evening, the father read the instructions of the door. He learned that it also had a normal key for problems with the power. However, the family kept that key on a shelf inside the house, so it did not help them at all. Now the key is with a friend who lives near them. "Every system that needs power should have a second solution," the father said, "and the second solution must not be inside the system."

#שאלה Why did the door not open that evening? | The power in the street went off | The family forgot the code | The code was changed | The door was broken | הדלת צריכה חשמל, והחשמל ברחוב נפל.
#שאלה Why did the normal key not help the family? | It was inside the house | It did not exist | It was broken | The father did not know the code | המפתח היה במגירה בתוך הבית, והם עמדו בחוץ.

#סיפור A Short PIN or a Long PIN? | בינוני
Many phones ask for a short PIN, such as 4821. With a short PIN like this, there are 10,000 possible PINs. With a long PIN, such as 482193, there are 1,000,000 possible PINs. So a long PIN is much harder to find.

However, a long PIN is not the only issue. Many people choose very simple PINs: 1234, 0000, 1111, or their birthday. Investigators who studied lost phones found that a few popular PINs could open a big part of the phones. A person with a stolen phone does not need to try 10,000 PINs; he only needs to try the most popular ones.

Most phones also stop for a few minutes after some invalid attempts, so a person with a stolen phone cannot try every possible PIN quickly. For this reason, the real risk is a simple PIN, not a short one. The investigators recommend a clear step: choose a long PIN that is not your birthday and not a simple sequence.
#שאלה How many possible PINs are there with a short PIN like 4821? | 10,000 | 4,821 | 1,000,000 | 1,234 | כתוב: there are 10,000 possible PINs.
#שאלה What did the investigators find? | A few popular PINs could open a big part of the phones | Long PINs are easy to find | People with stolen phones try 10,000 PINs | Most people choose long PINs | מעט קודים פופולריים פתחו חלק גדול מהטלפונים.
#שאלה Why can a person with a stolen phone not try every possible PIN quickly? | Most phones stop for a few minutes after some invalid attempts | PINs change every day | The phone deletes its data at once | Investigators watch the phone | הטלפון נעצר לכמה דקות אחרי כמה ניסיונות לא תקינים.
#שאלה Which PIN would the investigators recommend? | 739185 | 123456 | 000000 | a PIN with the birthday of the user | רק הראשון ארוך ולא פשוט. האחרים הם בדיוק מה שהטקסט מזהיר מפניו.

#סיפור The Evidence in the Database | בינוני
A small store noticed that money was missing from its system every Thursday. The store called an investigator, Noam. He studied the computer of the store and found evidence in the database: every Thursday at night, someone used the name and PIN of Ron to log in, the administrator of the store, and moved money.

But Ron did not steal the money. On those nights, he was on a trip far from the city, and pictures from his phone showed it. So another person knew his PIN.

Noam looked at the place near the computer and found the answer on the wall: a small paper with Ron's PIN on it. A person who worked in the store at night saw the paper and used the PIN. When the investigator showed him the evidence, he said that he was sorry and gave the money back. Ron now keeps his PIN only in his memory.
#שאלה What evidence did Noam find in the database? | Someone used Ron's name and PIN to log in every Thursday night | Ron moved money every Thursday | The database had malware | The store had no administrator | הראיה הראתה התחברות עם השם והקוד של רון, לא שרון עצמו עשה את זה.
#שאלה Why do we know that Ron did not steal the money? | He was on a trip far from the city on those nights | He had no PIN | He worked during the day | Noam was his friend | תמונות מהטלפון שלו הראו שהיה בטיול רחוק.
#שאלה How did the person who stole the money know the PIN? | It was on a small paper on the wall | He received it in a message | Ron told him | He found it in the database | הקוד היה על פתק קטן על הקיר ליד המחשב.
#שאלה What is the main lesson of the text? | A strong PIN is useless if other people can see it | Stores should not use computers | Administrators often steal money | Investigators should not use databases | הקוד עצמו לא היה הבעיה, אלא שהוא היה כתוב גלוי על הקיר.
